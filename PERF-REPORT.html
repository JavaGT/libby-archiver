TL;DR

Scope. Trial 2 of the continuous performance campaign. Trial 1 (perf-trial-20260906) merged: native CRC32, stored media + level-6 deflate in EPUBs, single-pass in-memory zip, streaming zip writer, lazy payload thunks, hash-while-streaming, keep-alive connection pooling, concurrent part/page/asset downloads, overlapped catalog fetches, latin1 byte loops for cfc1/descramble.
